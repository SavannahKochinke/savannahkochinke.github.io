I am a PhD student in Economics at Boston University. My research interests lie in labor economics, with a focus on the teacher labor force, K-12 education outcomes, and earnings inequities.

My current work examines the effects of teacher strikes on the teacher labor market. I hold Special Sworn Status (SSS) security clearance and have experience working with large Census administrative datasets, including the Longitudinal Employer-Household Dynamics (LEHD) data and Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (MA DESE) administrative records.
